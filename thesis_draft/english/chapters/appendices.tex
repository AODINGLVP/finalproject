\chapter{Planned Participant Study}
\label{app:study}

The prepared study contains six display conditions and three tasks per condition on the playable 241-node tree. A participant first completes three standardised practice tasks, then performs 18 counterbalanced formal trials. Six Action/path targets and six Decorator targets are balanced with condition order so that every method--target pair occurs twice across the planned 12 participants. The workbook contains 216 rows and records capped completion time, incorrect selections, zoom, pan, success, readability and cognitive load. The analysis script reports medians and quartiles, withholds inference below eight complete participant sets and applies the pre-registered repeated-measures tests only after real data are entered.

No participant rows have been collected. Before use, the author must obtain any required ethics approval, confirm the protocol with the supervisor, replace prototype instructions with participant-facing wording, and verify that consent and withdrawal procedures meet University requirements.
